Cheese Fries

Description

Cheddar cheese melted over fries.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. frozen french fries
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 6 oz. medium cheddar cheese freshly grated
  • 1/8 tsp ground cayenne pepper
  • kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Bake the fries according to the package directions.

  2. While the fries bake, melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add in the garlic and cook until lightly golden.

  3. Sprinkle over the flour and cook, whisking constantly, until the butter has absorbed all of the flour.

  4. While whisking, slowly stream in the milk, making sure to quickly break up and lumps.

  5. Continue to whisk until the sauce simmers and thickens, about 3-4 minutes. Remove from the heat.

  6. One handful at a time, add the cheese to the sauce. Stir until the cheese is completely melted before adding another handful. Continue until all the cheese is melted and the sauce is smooth.

  7. Whisk in the cayenne, salt, and pepper to taste.

  8. Arrange the fries on a serving platter. Top with the cheese sauce and garnishes, if desired. Serve immediately and enjoy!
